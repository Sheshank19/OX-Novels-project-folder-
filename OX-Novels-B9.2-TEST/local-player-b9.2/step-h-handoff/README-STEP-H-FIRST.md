# OX B9.2 Step H — LOCAL SEALED Step E -> PRIVATE Step G handoff

**Scope: private files on your Windows PC ONLY.** This patch includes NO Cloudflare deployment, NO Google Drive request, NO Apps Script call, NO KV/D1 operation, NO catalog publication, and NO Player modification.

## What Step H adds

The earlier Step G inspector could validate a changed Step E candidate in memory. **Step H now saves** a *separately verified, immutable local bundle* in the exact Step G chunk/manifest format, in preparation for a later, separately approved staging-only transfer. It **does not transfer or activate** anything.

It requires the real, matching pair generated by `B9.2-STEP-E-REAL-DRIVE-ONCE.bat` **after a legitimate content change**:

- `quarantine-....PRIVATE-CATALOG.json` (actual encrypted track tokens, but names are readable)
- `quarantine-....PRIVATE-RECEIPT.json` (HMAC-sealed evidence; explicitly NOT publication authorization)

This new code checks the saved trusted baseline, the exact Step E receipt HMAC and catalog SHA-256, current static Player catalog digest, existing Player encryption key/token validation, folder/track counts, and actual semantic change. An absent receipt, altered input, inconsistent baseline, wrong key, unknown pair, or stale static catalog is **rejected**. Two matching scans are still NOT an atomic Google Drive snapshot.

A successful handoff is saved in:

`local-player-b9.2\staging\_PRIVATE_STEP_H_BUNDLE_NO_UPLOAD\bundle-<SHA256>\`

With `MANIFEST.json`, `DO-NOT-UPLOAD.txt`, and `PRIVATE-CHUNKS\000.PRIVATE-CHUNK.bin`, etc. The manifest contains only hashed/size/count metadata; the **chunk files contain private catalog information**. Do not share, publish, deploy, or sync that folder to public cloud storage. On a rerun, a verified identical bundle is reused; existing corrupted files are NOT overwritten.
