Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

OX Novels respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use oxnovels.top, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use OX Novels, we may collect information such as your name and email address when you sign in through Google, account-related information, membership or purchase records, subscription identifiers, payment status, and customer-support communications.

We may also receive limited technical information necessary to operate and protect the website, such as IP address, browser or device information, login activity, and basic website logs.

2. Google Sign-In

OX Novels uses Google sign-in to create and access customer accounts. When you sign in with Google, we may receive information provided by Google such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

We do not receive or store your Google password. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed through third-party payment providers. OX Novels may receive information related to a transaction, including payment status, transaction or subscription identifiers, purchase amount, billing status, and other information necessary to confirm and manage access.

We do not normally receive or store your full credit or debit card number. Payment providers process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to provide and manage OX Novels accounts, confirm purchases and memberships, provide access to purchased stories, process membership status changes, prevent fraud or unauthorized access, provide customer support, investigate payment or access problems, maintain website security, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to operating and supporting OX Novels without an appropriate reason or permission.

5. Cookies and Website Data

OX Novels may use cookies or similar browser storage to keep users signed in, maintain sessions, remember necessary settings, and provide website functionality.

Some third-party services used by OX Novels may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Removing website cookies or site data may sign you out of your OX Novels account.

6. Sharing of Information

OX Novels does not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers where necessary to operate OX Novels, including authentication providers, payment processors, website or database hosting providers, email or support services, and security or infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or where reasonably necessary to protect OX Novels, its users, or the security of the service.

7. Third-Party Services

OX Novels relies on third-party services for certain features. These may include services used for Google authentication, payment processing, website hosting, databases, content delivery, email, and other technical operations.

Those companies may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

We may retain account, purchase, membership, and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide purchased access, maintain accurate payment records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and operate the service.

Some transaction information may need to be retained even after an account is no longer actively used.
