What to Include

When contacting support, please include enough information for us to identify the issue, such as:

  • The email address connected to your OX Novels account.
  • The name of the story or membership involved.
  • A brief description of the problem.
  • A screenshot of any error message, if applicable.

Keep Sensitive Information Private

For security, please do not send passwords, full card details, PayPal passwords, payment-provider passwords, or other sensitive login information.

Payment or Access Problems

For payment or access problems, please contact us before making another payment so we can first check your existing purchase.

We will review support requests and respond as soon as reasonably possible.
