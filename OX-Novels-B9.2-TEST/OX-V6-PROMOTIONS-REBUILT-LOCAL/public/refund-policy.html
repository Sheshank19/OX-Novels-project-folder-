OX NOVELS POLICIES

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

At OX Novels, we provide access to digital audio-story content through recurring memberships and one-time Lifetime Story Access purchases.

Digital Purchases and Refunds

Because our products are digital and access is provided shortly after successful payment, purchases are generally non-refundable once access to the purchased content or membership has been provided.

Please review the story, membership option, price, and other available information carefully before completing your purchase.

A refund may still be considered in situations such as:

  • A duplicate payment was charged by mistake.
  • Payment was successfully completed but access was not provided because of a technical problem on our side.
  • An incorrect charge occurred due to a payment-processing error.
  • A refund is required under applicable consumer law.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify the payment and investigate the issue before approving a refund.

Membership Cancellations

OX Novels memberships are recurring subscriptions. You may cancel your subscription through the payment provider used to process the subscription to prevent future renewals.

After cancellation, your membership access will normally continue until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically provide a refund for the current or previous billing periods. Unless required by applicable law, we do not provide partial or prorated refunds for unused time remaining in a membership period.

Lifetime Story Access

Lifetime Story Access is a one-time purchase for access to the individual story selected during checkout. It is not a recurring subscription and does not automatically renew.

Because access to the digital story is provided after purchase, Lifetime Story Access purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been granted, except for the circumstances described above or where required by law.

Purchasing Lifetime Story Access for one story does not provide Lifetime Access to other stories on OX Novels.

Technical Problems

If you have paid but cannot access your purchased content, please contact us before making another payment.

We will first try to restore or correct your access. If we cannot provide the purchased digital content because of a problem on our side, we may provide an appropriate solution or refund where applicable.

Payment Processing

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers. Refunds, where approved, may be returned through the original payment method and can also be subject to the payment provider's processing rules and timelines.
